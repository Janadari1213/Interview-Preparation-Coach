## Non-Verbal Communication and Body Language Guidance

### Maintaining Effective Eye Contact
Eye contact builds trust and demonstrates engagement during interviews. In in-person settings, maintain natural eye contact with all interviewers rather than staring continuously. For virtual interviews, look directly into the camera lens when speaking to simulate direct eye contact.

### Posture and Physical Presence
Sit upright with a slight forward lean to show attentiveness and enthusiasm. Keep your shoulders relaxed and hands open, avoiding crossed arms or defensive postures. Controlled hand gestures can emphasize key points without becoming distracting.

### Tone of Voice and Pace
Speak clearly at a moderate pace, allowing natural pauses to gather your thoughts before answering complex questions. Modulate your tone to convey enthusiasm and confidence while avoiding monotone delivery or rushed explanations.

### Virtual Interview Environment Setup
Ensure your camera is positioned at eye level, lighting is bright and front-facing, and your background is uncluttered and professional. Test your microphone and audio connection beforehand to prevent technical friction during the interview.
