## STAR Method Framework for Behavioral Interviews
**Topic:** Behavioral Interviewing
**Description:** Structural guide to answering behavioral questions effectively using Situation, Task, Action, and Result.

### What is the STAR Method?
The STAR method is a structured technique used to answer behavioral interview questions by discussing a specific event or situation. It stands for:
- **Situation:** Set the context and describe the background of the event or challenge.
- **Task:** Explain your specific responsibility or role in addressing the situation.
- **Action:** Detail the concrete steps you personally took to overcome the challenge or complete the goal.
- **Result:** Share the outcome, quantifying achievements and highlighting key lessons learned.

### Practical Example
**Question:** "Describe a time you solved a critical technical issue under a tight deadline."
- **Situation:** During a major deployment at my previous internship, our API latency spiked by 300% right before launch.
- **Task:** As the backend engineer on duty, I needed to identify the bottleneck and restore normal latency within 2 hours.
- **Action:** I analyzed server logs, identified an unindexed database query in the user authentication module, created a compound index, and deployed a hotfix.
- **Result:** Latency dropped by 80% below baseline, and the system launched on schedule with zero downtime for initial users.
