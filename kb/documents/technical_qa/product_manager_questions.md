## Q: What is the RICE prioritization framework and how is it calculated?

**Topic:** Product Prioritization & Strategy  
**Role:** Product Manager  
**Difficulty:** medium  
**Q:** What is the RICE prioritization framework and how is it calculated?  
**A:** RICE evaluates feature backlog items using four factors: **Reach** (users impacted per time period), **Impact** (degree of benefit per user: massive=3, high=2, medium=1, low=0.5), **Confidence** (percentage certainty of estimates), and **Effort** (person-months required). Formula: $RICE\ Score = \frac{Reach \times Impact \times Confidence}{Effort}$. Higher scores indicate higher priority features.

## Q: What is the difference between DAU, MAU, and the DAU/MAU Stickiness Ratio?

**Topic:** Product Metrics & Analytics  
**Role:** Product Manager  
**Difficulty:** easy  
**Q:** What is the difference between DAU, MAU, and the DAU/MAU Stickiness Ratio?  
**A:** **DAU** (Daily Active Users) measures unique users engaging with the product in 24 hours. **MAU** (Monthly Active Users) measures unique users over 30 days. The **DAU/MAU ratio** (expressed as a percentage) measures product stickiness — indicating how frequently monthly users return on a daily basis (e.g., 20%+ is considered good stickiness for SaaS).

## Q: How do you handle conflicting feature requests from key stakeholders vs end users?

**Topic:** Stakeholder & Roadmap Management  
**Role:** Product Manager  
**Difficulty:** hard  
**Q:** How do you handle conflicting feature requests from key stakeholders vs end users?  
**A:** 1) Align both parties around overarching business objectives and strategic KPIs. 2) Gather quantitative data (analytics/surveys) and qualitative user feedback to validate user demand. 3) Map stakeholder requests against business value vs technical effort. 4) Use framework-backed trade-offs (e.g., RICE or Kano model) to communicate transparent prioritization decisions without burning bridges.

## Q: What is a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) and what is its primary objective?

**Topic:** Product Discovery & MVP  
**Role:** Product Manager  
**Difficulty:** easy  
**Q:** What is a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) and what is its primary objective?  
**A:** An MVP is the simplest version of a product released to early adopters with just enough features to validate core hypotheses. Its primary objective is accelerated learning with minimal development effort, gathering real-world user feedback to iterate or pivot before committing heavy engineering resources.

## Q: How do you measure and reduce customer churn rate?

**Topic:** Retention & Customer Success  
**Role:** Product Manager  
**Difficulty:** medium  
**Q:** How do you measure and reduce customer churn rate?  
**A:** **Churn Rate** = $\frac{\text{Users Lost in Period}}{\text{Users at Start of Period}} \times 100\%$. Reduction strategies: 1) Analyze drop-off funnels to identify onboarding friction. 2) Conduct exit interviews to uncover root churn drivers. 3) Build proactive engagement triggers for inactive accounts. 4) Improve feature discovery and customer success onboarding pathways.

## Q: What is a User Persona and how does it inform product decisions?

**Topic:** Product Discovery & User Research  
**Role:** Product Manager  
**Difficulty:** easy  
**Q:** What is a User Persona and how does it inform product decisions?  
**A:** A User Persona is a semi-fictional archetype representing a target user segment based on empirical research, demographics, pain points, behaviors, and goals. It grounds team alignment during feature design, preventing bias by ensuring product decisions solve concrete user problems rather than internal assumptions.

## Q: What steps do you follow during a Product Launch Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy?

**Topic:** Go-To-Market Strategy  
**Role:** Product Manager  
**Difficulty:** hard  
**Q:** What steps do you follow during a Product Launch Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy?  
**A:** 1) **Value Proposition**: Define target audience, positioning, and key messaging. 2) **Cross-functional Alignment**: Coordinate product, marketing, sales, and support readiness. 3) **Phased Rollout**: Run internal testing -> beta release -> public launch. 4) **Success Tracking**: Monitor adoption rates, user feedback, customer support tickets, and revenue impact post-launch.

## Q: What is the Kano Model and how does it categorize customer requirements?

**Topic:** Product Discovery & Prioritization  
**Role:** Product Manager  
**Difficulty:** medium  
**Q:** What is the Kano Model and how does it categorize customer requirements?  
**A:** The Kano Model classifies features into 5 categories based on customer emotional response: 1) **Basic Needs** (must-haves taken for granted). 2) **Performance Needs** (more is better, proportional satisfaction). 3) **Delighters** (unexpected features causing high excitement). 4) **Indifferent** (users don't care). 5) **Reverse** (dissatisfies users if present).

## Q: How do you prioritize technical debt against new feature development?

**Topic:** Engineering Collaboration  
**Role:** Product Manager  
**Difficulty:** hard  
**Q:** How do you prioritize technical debt against new feature development?  
**A:** Allocate a consistent capacity budget per sprint (e.g., 20% for refactoring/tech debt, 80% for product features). Evaluate tech debt using risk and impact metrics: Does unaddressed debt degrade system latency, cause downtime, or slow feature velocity? Partner with engineering leads to quantify tech debt repayment value in business terms.

## Q: What is the difference between Product Vision, Product Strategy, and Product Roadmap?

**Topic:** Product Leadership  
**Role:** Product Manager  
**Difficulty:** medium  
**Q:** What is the difference between Product Vision, Product Strategy, and Product Roadmap?  
**A:** **Product Vision**: The ultimate long-term aspiration of the product (Why it exists). **Product Strategy**: The strategic path chosen to achieve the vision, including target market and key differentiators (How we win). **Product Roadmap**: An execution timeline of high-level goals and feature milestones delivering the strategy (What we build and when).
