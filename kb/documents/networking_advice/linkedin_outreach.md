## Strategic Prospecting and Target Identification on LinkedIn

**Topic:** LinkedIn Prospecting  
**Role:** General  
**Category:** Strategic Advice  

### High-Conversion Prospecting Strategy
When seeking industry connections on LinkedIn, focus on professionals who are 2 to 5 years ahead of your current career stage (e.g., Senior Engineers, Tech Leads, Senior Data Analysts, Senior PMs). They are close enough to remember the entry-level job search struggle and are frequently involved in interview panels and referral recommendations.

### Pre-Outreach Profile Research
Never send a connection request without reviewing the practitioner's recent activity. Check their recent posts, articles, patents, open-source contributions, or shared company projects. Reference a specific technical project or shared interest to differentiate your message from automated recruiter spam.

## The 3-Point Connection Hook Strategy

**Topic:** Connection Strategy  
**Role:** General  
**Category:** Strategic Advice  

### The 300-Character Note Rule
Keep LinkedIn connection request notes strictly under 300 characters. Follow the 3-point hook structure:
1. **The Common Ground:** Mention a shared university background, company alumni status, or specific technical article.
2. **The Specific Admiration:** Highlight a specific engineering post or project they authored.
3. **The Non-Transactional Call to Connect:** Express a genuine desire to follow their professional updates without asking for job favors or immediate referrals in the first touchpoint.
