## Strategic LinkedIn Networking & Cold Outreach Guide

### Target Identification and Research
Before reaching out to industry professionals on LinkedIn, research their profile, recent posts, and shared connections. Prioritize company alumni, mutual contacts, or practitioners working in your specific area of interest (e.g., Software Engineering, Data Science).

### Personalized Connection Requests
Always include a personalized note with your connection request. Mention a specific detail from their career trajectory, an article they wrote, or a shared university background to establish immediate relevance and differentiate your message from automated spam.

### Value-Driven Relationship Building
Approach outreach with a mindset of learning rather than asking for immediate favors or job referrals. Request brief informational chats (15-20 minutes) to discuss their career journey, industry trends, or advice for aspiring graduates. Always send a warm thank-you note following any interaction.
