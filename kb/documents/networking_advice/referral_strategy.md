## Earning Internal Job Referrals Naturally

**Topic:** Job Referral Strategy  
**Role:** General  
**Category:** Strategic Advice  

### The Organic Referral Pathway
Direct cold requests for job referrals (e.g., "Hi, please refer me for job ID 12345") have an extremely low response rate because employees risk their internal reputation by referring unknown applicants. To earn referrals organically:
1. **Establish Prior Contact:** Engage with the contact through an informational interview or mutual discussion first.
2. **Demonstrate Role Fit:** Share a short summary of how your skills and past project outcomes align directly with the open requisition requirements.
3. **Make Referral Easy:** Provide your resume PDF, requisition ID, and a 3-bullet summary of your key achievements that the contact can forward directly to their internal recruiting portal.

## Engaging Engineering Leads and Hiring Managers

**Topic:** Hiring Manager Outreach  
**Role:** General  
**Category:** Strategic Advice  

### Direct Outreach to Engineering Leads
When reaching out directly to hiring managers or engineering leads regarding an active job opening:
- **Lead with Value and Projects:** Highlight 1 relevant technical project, open-source repo, or live demo that directly relates to the team's tech stack.
- **Keep it Concise:** Busy engineering managers prioritize concise messages that demonstrate problem-solving initiative over long cover letters.
- **Follow Up Respectfully:** If you don't receive a response within 5 to 7 business days, send 1 polite follow-up reiterating your enthusiasm.
